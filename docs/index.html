About vinStelX

Space infrastructure for the data age.

vinStelX builds orbital infrastructure that brings compute closer to where data is created. We believe the next generation of satellite constellations shouldn't be limited by what they can send back to Earth.

Vinsta Compute is our first product — a modular orbital edge platform for operators who need real-time processing and sovereign control over on-orbit data.

Why Vinsta Compute

The bottleneck is on the ground.

Satellites generate more data than downlinks can carry. Every terabyte sent to Earth costs time, bandwidth, and money — and delays the decisions that matter.

Process at the source.

Run workloads in orbit. Downlink insights, not raw imagery. Decide in real time, where the data is born — with full control over what leaves the constellation.
