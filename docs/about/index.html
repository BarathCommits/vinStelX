About

Building the orbital edge

We're a space infrastructure company focused on bringing compute to where satellite data is born.

Our story

Space infrastructure for the data age.

vinStelX was founded on a simple observation: satellite constellations are generating more data than they can efficiently return to Earth. The bottleneck isn't in space — it's in how we process and move that data.

We're building the orbital infrastructure layer that brings compute to the edge of space. Our mission is to enable constellation operators to process, store, and infer at the source — reducing latency, bandwidth costs, and dependency on ground availability.

Vinsta Compute is our first product, with more orbital systems on the roadmap as we scale from demonstrator missions to production constellation deployments.

Our principles

What guides us.

  • Compute where data lives

    We build infrastructure at the source of data creation — not downstream on the ground.

  • Sovereign by design

    Operators control what is processed, stored, and transmitted from orbit.

  • Built to scale

    Modular nodes designed to grow from demonstrator missions to full constellations.
