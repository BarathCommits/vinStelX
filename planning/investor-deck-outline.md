## Slide 2 — Problem

**Satellites generate more data than Earth can absorb.**

| Bottleneck | Impact |
|------------|--------|
| Ground-station windows | Minutes to hours of latency per decision |
| Downlink bandwidth | $/GB scales with raw imagery and IoT floods |
| Ground processing | Tied to pass schedules, not mission urgency |
| Sovereignty | Sensitive payloads routed through third parties |

> Constellation operators are data-rich and insight-poor.

---

## Slide 3 — Solution

**In-Orbit Data Centres — compute where satellite data is born.**

Three-step pipeline (already on product page):

1. **Ingest** — Payload sensors + inter-satellite crosslinks feed the orbital node
2. **Process & store** — Edge servers run workloads, buffer, tier, archive on-board
3. **Selective downlink** — Encrypted, policy-controlled delivery of insights only

**Platform name:** Vinsta Compute  
**Deployment:** Modular LEO nodes (400–600 km), radiation-hardened, mesh-ready

---

## Slide 4 — Why now

- LEO constellation count and data rates are scaling faster than ground segment investment
- On-board AI accelerators and optical crosslinks are commercially viable
- Defense and sovereign operators require in-orbit processing, not ground relay
- Downlink cost and latency are now P&L line items for EO and IoT operators
